# Hostel Rules

## Hostel In-Time

The hostel in-time is 9:00 PM.

Students must return to the hostel by 9:00 PM. Exceptions require approval from the appropriate hostel authority.

## General Rules

Students must follow hostel discipline and the instructions issued by the hostel administration.

## Late Return

Students who return after the prescribed in-time may be subject to the hostel administration's disciplinary procedures.
