# Placement Eligibility Policy

## Academic Eligibility

- Minimum 7.0 CGPA with no active backlogs.

## Attendance

- Minimum 85% attendance in all courses.

## Skills

- Completion of at least 2 technical projects.
- Participation in at least 1 technical competition or hackathon.

## Documents

- Updated resume.
- GitHub profile with project repositories.

## Disqualification

Students with disciplinary actions may be disqualified from placement activities.
