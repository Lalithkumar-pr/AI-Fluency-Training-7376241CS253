# Examination Attendance Regulations

## Minimum Attendance

Students with attendance of 75% or more are eligible to appear for the examination, subject to other applicable institutional requirements.

## Condonation

Students whose attendance is at least 65% but below 75% may apply for condonation.

The condonation fee is ₹500 per course.

Condonation is not automatic. Approval from the appropriate authority is required.

## Below Minimum Attendance

Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible to appear for the examination under these rules.

## Examples

- 82% attendance: eligible under the attendance rule.
- 70% attendance: condonation application required; fee ₹500 per course.
- 50% attendance: not eligible under the attendance rule.
